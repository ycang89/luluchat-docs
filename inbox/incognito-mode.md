# Incognito Mode

## What is Incognito Mode?

Incognito Mode lets you read chats in the Inbox without marking them as read. When it is on, opening a chat with unread messages does not clear its unread count, so the chat still shows as unread for you and your teammates.

The toggle sits at the top right of the Inbox, next to your lists. It shows **Incognito Off** or **Incognito On**.

## When to use it?

* **Reviewing chats**: Check what a customer said without changing the chat's status.
* **Supervising**: Managers can look through teammates' chats while the chats stay unread for the person who handles them.
* **Gathering context**: Read the history first, then reply when you are ready.

## How to use it (Step by Step)

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
#### Turn on Incognito

In the Inbox, click **Incognito Off** at the top right. The button changes to **Incognito On** and is highlighted.

{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Open the chats you want to read

Click any chat in the chat list. You can read the messages, but the chat keeps its unread badge.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Turn Incognito off when you are done

Click **Incognito On** to switch it back to **Incognito Off**. From now on, opening an unread chat marks it as read again.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

## What happens after it triggers?

* Opening an unread chat does not mark it as read.
* New messages that arrive in the chat you have open are not marked as read either.
* Everything else works as usual. You can still reply, tag, assign and close chats.

## Important behavior to know

* **It only affects "read" status.** Incognito does not hide your replies, typing or other actions. Anything you send is sent as normal.
* **It is saved in your browser.** The setting is remembered on this browser and device until you change it. It does not apply to other browsers, devices or teammates.
* **You can still mark chats as read yourself.** Use **Mark as Read** from the chat row menu (see [Conversation Controls](conversation-controls.md)) or [Bulk Actions](bulk-actions.md).

## Common issues & solutions

* **A chat was marked as read even though Incognito was on**: Check that the button shows **Incognito On**. If you use another browser or device, turn it on there too.
* **Chats stay unread after I finish reviewing**: Incognito is probably still on. Click **Incognito On** to turn it off.
* **I can't find the toggle**: It is at the top right of the Inbox list bar. On small screens, the label may be hidden and only the icon shows.

## Best practice 💡

* Turn Incognito on before you open the chat, not after.
* Turn it off before you start your normal work, so chats you handle are marked as read.
* Use it for reviews and audits, not for daily replying.
