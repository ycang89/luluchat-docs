# Sound Settings (Mute/Unmute)

## What are Sound Settings?

The Inbox plays a sound when a new message arrives and when you send a message. The **Sound On** / **Sound Muted** button at the top right of the Inbox turns these sounds on or off.

## When to use it?

* **Focus time**: Mute the Inbox while you work on something else.
* **Live support**: Keep sound on so you hear new messages right away.
* **Shared spaces**: Mute sounds in meetings or quiet offices.

## How to use it (Step by Step)

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
#### Open the Inbox

Click `Inbox` from the left menu.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Click the sound button

At the top right, next to the Incognito button, click **Sound On** to mute. The button changes to **Sound Muted** with a crossed-out speaker icon.

{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Turn sound back on

Click **Sound Muted** to switch back to **Sound On**. Hover over the button to see a hint: "Click to mute Inbox Sound" or "Click to unmute Inbox Sound".
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

## What happens after it triggers?

* **Sound On**: You hear a sound for each new incoming message, and a sound when you send a message.
* **Sound Muted**: The Inbox stays silent. Messages still arrive and the chat list still updates.

## Important behavior to know

* **Saved in your browser.** The setting is remembered on this browser and device. It does not change sounds for your teammates or on your other devices.
* **Muted chats stay silent.** If you muted a single chat (see [Conversation Controls](conversation-controls.md)), it does not play a sound even when Inbox sound is on.
* **Only incoming messages from contacts play the new-message sound.** Messages you or your team send do not.
* This button controls Inbox sounds only. It does not change browser or mobile push notifications.

## Common issues & solutions

* **No sound after unmuting**: Check your computer volume and that the browser tab is not muted. Some browsers block sound until you have clicked somewhere on the page.
* **No sound for one chat**: That chat may be muted. Open the chat row menu and choose **Unmute**.
* **Still hearing sounds when muted**: Make sure the button shows **Sound Muted**. If you have Luluchat open in another browser or tab with sound on, mute it there too.

## Best practice 💡

* Mute during focused work, and unmute when you handle live chats.
* Mute noisy single chats instead of muting the whole Inbox.
