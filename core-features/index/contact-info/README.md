# Contact Info

## What is Contact Info?

The **Contact Info** panel sits on the right side of a conversation in `Inbox`. It shows everything about the contact you're chatting with, so you can update their details, leave notes for your team and follow up on deals, tickets and flows without leaving the chat.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/inbox-overview.png" alt="Inbox with the Contact Info panel on the right showing Profile, Notes, Flows and Tickets tabs"><figcaption><p>Contact Info panel</p></figcaption></figure>

## When to use it?

* When you need to assign the conversation to a teammate or add collaborators.
* When you want to tag the contact, update their name, remarks or opt-in status.
* When you want to check or create a deal or ticket for the contact.
* When you want to see which message flows are running for the contact, or send one.
* When you want to leave an internal note for your team.

## How to use it (Step by Step)

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
#### Open the panel

In `Inbox`, open a conversation. Click the **Contact Info** icon (the person icon) at the top right of the conversation header. You can also click the contact's name in the header.

On a phone, tap the three dots at the top right and choose **Contact Info**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/inbox-conversation-header.png" alt="Conversation header; the person icon on the far right opens Contact Info"><figcaption><p>The Contact Info (person) icon</p></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Pick a tab

The panel has these tabs:

| Tab | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| [**Profile**](../../../inbox/contact-info/profile.md) | AI Agent, Assignee, Collaborator, Tags, [Deals](../../../inbox/contact-info/deals.md), profile details (name, number, opt-in status, remarks) and Custom Attributes. |
| [**Notes**](../../../inbox/contact-info/notes.md) | Internal notes about this contact. The number shows how many notes there are. |
| [**Flows**](../../../inbox/contact-info/flows.md) | Message flows and smart delay messages running for this contact. You can also send a message flow from here. |
| [**Tickets**](../../../inbox/contact-info/tickets.md) | Tickets linked to this contact. |

The **Tickets** tab and the **Deals** section only appear if your account has access to Tickets and Deals.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Manage the contact's media

Click the **Manage Storage** icon (the folder icon) at the top of the panel to see all media files shared in this conversation. See [Profile](../../../inbox/contact-info/profile.md) for details.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Close the panel

Click the **X** at the top right of the panel, or click the **Contact Info** icon again.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

## What happens after it triggers?

Changes you make in the panel are saved to the contact straight away and are visible to your teammates. The same contact details also appear in `Contacts`.

## Important behavior to know

* **New contacts**: If a contact has just messaged you for the first time, the panel may say you need some conversation first. Luluchat may still be checking the contact on WhatsApp. Click **Reload** to try again.
* **Permissions**: If your admin has restricted you from editing contact profiles, you can view the Profile tab but can't change it.
* **The panel stays open** as you move between conversations, so you can go through chats quickly.

## Common issues & solutions

* **Contact Info icon is missing**: The contact has not been created yet. Wait for the conversation to start, then open the panel from the contact's name and click **Reload**.
* **Tickets tab or Deals section is missing**: Your plan or role does not include Tickets or Deals. Ask your admin.
* **Fields are greyed out**: Your permission to edit contacts has been restricted. Ask your admin.

## Best practice 💡

* Keep the panel open while you reply, so you can set the assignee and tags as you go.
* Check the **Notes** and **Flows** tabs before replying, so you know what your team or automations have already done.
